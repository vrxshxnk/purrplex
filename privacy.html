Purrplex privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Purrplex is a puzzle game about two cats. It collects no personal information: there is no account to make, and the game never asks for your name, email or anything else about you. On phones and tablets it counts, anonymously, how the game is played, so we can see where players get stuck (below), and you can turn that off. The phone and tablet versions show ads from Google between rooms, and ads you choose to watch for a reward (below); one purchase removes the ads between rooms. The web version has no ads.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the game keeps a small save on your device: the rooms you have solved and your best scores, your fish, boosters, hearts and daily streak, what the cats wear, how fond they are of you, your settings, and any captions you write on your photos. It stays on your device and you can remove it by deleting the app.

Backups

  • iPhone and iPad: a copy of the save is kept in your own iCloud Keychain, so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game or move to a new device. It is stored in your Apple account, end-to-end encrypted, and we cannot see it.
  • Android: Android's own Auto Backup may copy the save to your Google account, if you have backup turned on. It is handled by Google, and we cannot see it.
  • On the web: the save stays in your browser only.

Anonymous counts

On iPhone, iPad and Android, the game counts how it is played: which rooms are started, solved, failed or left, how many swipes, undos and hints they took, when the game goes to the background in the middle of a room, what is bought with fish, and how often players come back. Each count carries a random number made for the app on your device, and the basic facts that come with any app's counts (the device model, its system version, the game's version and the country, worked out from the network). It never carries your name, email, contacts, precise location or your device's advertising identifier, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites or to choose ads.

The counts are gathered for us by Google Firebase Analytics, under Google's privacy policy. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings ("Anonymous counts"). The web version counts nothing.

Ads

On iPhone, iPad and Android, the game shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between rooms, and ads you choose to watch for something in the game (hearts back, more swipes, double fish, a Streak Shield). AdMob, not the game, picks and shows each ad, and to do that it collects information from your device as any app's ads do: an identifier for the device, its IP address and the rough location that comes from it, and how you interact with the ads, together with diagnostic information. Google describes this in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

On iPhone and iPad every ad is non-personalised: the game never asks to track you, and the ads are not chosen from what you do in other apps or websites. On Android, ads may be personalised unless you turn that off in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most phones). In the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form asks you first, and "Ad privacy choices" in the game's Settings lets you change your answer.

Remove ads is a one-time purchase, handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play: the game learns only whether it was bought, never your payment details. It removes the ads between rooms; the ads you choose to watch stay, if you want them.

Reminders

If you say yes, the game schedules reminders for the daily room on your device. They are local notifications: no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share (your daily result, or a polaroid as a GIF), the game hands it to your device's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. "Report a problem" in Settings works the same way: it prepares a short text (the game's version, your device and screen, your settings, the last room you played and any recent errors) and sends nothing unless you send it yourself.

Children

Purrplex is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, children included: the anonymous counts above carry none.
