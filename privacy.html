Purrplex privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Purrplex is a puzzle game about two cats. It collects no personal information: there is no account to make, and the game never asks for your name, email or anything else about you. On phones and tablets it counts, anonymously, how the game is played, so we can see where players get stuck (below), and you can turn that off. There is no advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the game keeps a small save on your device: the rooms you have solved and your best scores, your fish, boosters, hearts and daily streak, what the cats wear, how fond they are of you, your settings, and any captions you write on your photos. It stays on your device and you can remove it by deleting the app.

Backups

  • iPhone and iPad: a copy of the save is kept in your own iCloud Keychain, so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game or move to a new device. It is stored in your Apple account, end-to-end encrypted, and we cannot see it.
  • Android: Android's own Auto Backup may copy the save to your Google account, if you have backup turned on. It is handled by Google, and we cannot see it.
  • On the web: the save stays in your browser only.

Anonymous counts

On iPhone, iPad and Android, the game counts how it is played: which rooms are started, solved, failed or left, how many swipes, undos and hints they took, when the game goes to the background in the middle of a room, what is bought with fish, and how often players come back. Each count carries a random number made for the app on your device, and the basic facts that come with any app's counts (the device model, its system version, the game's version and the country, worked out from the network). It never carries your name, email, contacts, precise location or your device's advertising identifier, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites or to show you ads.

The counts are gathered for us by Google Firebase Analytics, under Google's privacy policy. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings ("Anonymous counts"). The web version counts nothing.

Reminders

If you say yes, the game schedules reminders for the daily room on your device. They are local notifications: no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share (your daily result, or a polaroid as a GIF), the game hands it to your device's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. "Report a problem" in Settings works the same way: it prepares a short text (the game's version, your device and screen, your settings, the last room you played and any recent errors) and sends nothing unless you send it yourself.

Children

Purrplex is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included: the anonymous counts above carry none.
